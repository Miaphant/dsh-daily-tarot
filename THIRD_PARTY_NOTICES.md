# Third-party artwork

The MIT license applies to this plugin's source code. It does not relicense the tarot artwork.

The 22 card images are reproductions of the original Rider–Waite–Smith major arcana, illustrated by **Pamela Colman Smith**, conceived with **Arthur Edward Waite**, originally published in 1909. The original artwork is public domain as indicated by Wikimedia Commons. The plugin uses the original JPG scans rather than modern recolorings.

Sources: [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Rider-Waite_tarot_deck). Exact per-card source and description URLs are recorded in [SOURCES.json](SOURCES.json).

The bundled images were proportionally resized to a maximum dimension of 900 pixels for local display. Reversed cards are rotated at runtime; the underlying artwork is otherwise unchanged.

DeepSeek Harness and React are provided by the user's host application, not bundled with this plugin. This is an independent community project, unaffiliated with and not endorsed by DeepSeek.
